How each report was placed

  • By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake guesses parser or hint from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), and the two disagree often. The table ignores the tag.
  • parser. Every tenant on #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: a booking for 4 March lands on 3 April. Only a date with a day of 12 or less can swap, which is why tenants see it on some bookings and not others.
  • hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, so the date swaps before the parser sees it. Only reports sent through the form, from tenants on booking-v3. Fixing the parser does not fix this; the hint must follow the tenant’s locale.
  • one-day. The date is not swapped; it moves by a day (“Tuesday 12 May” shows as “Monday 11 May”), or the same meeting shows different days to people in different time zones. The desk had already read these reports this way (desk-notes.json), but the #4471 page counts them under parser as a footnote. They need their own ticket: a parser fix will not move them back.
  • Repeats. The ticket export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Each report is counted once, by its id.
